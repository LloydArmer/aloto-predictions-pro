DESCRIPTION
ALOTO Prediction Pro runs the football score prediction league you have been keeping in a spreadsheet. Set up a competition, share the join code with your group, and the app handles the rest — locking predictions at kick-off, scoring every result, and keeping the table up to date.

HOW IT WORKS
One person creates a competition and runs it. Everyone else joins with a six-character code. Add your fixtures each gameweek, enter the results when the matches finish, and the points, tables and bonuses resolve themselves.

SCORING
Two points for the correct result, five for the exact score, and bonus points for a full house of results or scorelines across a gameweek. Every value is yours to change.

TRIPLE POINTS
Two chips a season, one either side of New Year. Play one and everything you score that gameweek is tripled.

LEAGUES, CUPS AND TOURNAMENTS
Run a season-long league, a knockout cup drawn on gameweek scores, or a group stage feeding into a bracket. Run all three at once if your group has the appetite.

SEASON PREDICTIONS
Call the final league table in August and back your picks for the season's winners. Everything stays sealed until the deadline, then opens for the whole group to compare.

LIVE SCORES ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
Your gameweek total updates as the goals go in, without opening the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Competitions are joined by code only. There is no public directory, no global leaderboard, and no way for anyone outside your group to find your competition. Predictions stay hidden from everyone, including the admin, until each match kicks off.

FREE TO PLAY
Playing is always free, however many competitions you are in, and there are no adverts anywhere in the app.

ALOTO PRO — £29.99 a year
For organisers who run more than one competition. Pro adds unlimited leagues and cups, individual season predictions including questions you write yourself, and a full archive of past seasons. The free plan includes one league, one cup and the final table prediction.
