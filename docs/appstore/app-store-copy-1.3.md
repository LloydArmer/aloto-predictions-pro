# ALOTO Prediction Pro — App Store listing copy for 1.3

Everything below is ready to paste into App Store Connect. Character limits are
noted where Apple enforces one.

---

## 1. App Name (30 characters max)

**Recommended:**

    ALOTO: Football Predictions

27 characters.

The current name is "ALOTO Prediction Pro". The name is the strongest thing
Apple's search ranks on, and yours currently spends all of it on words nobody
searches for: "ALOTO" means nothing to a stranger, and "Prediction Pro" does not
say football. Adding the word Football is the single biggest search change
available to you.

Keeping "ALOTO Prediction Pro" is a reasonable choice if you would rather the
name matched the website and the subscription. It costs you search traffic, not
correctness.

---

## 2. Subtitle (30 characters max)

**Recommended:**

    Prediction leagues for friends

29 characters. This is already close to what you have — it works, and with
"Football" moved into the name the subtitle no longer has to carry it.

---

## 3. Keywords (100 characters max, comma separated, no spaces after commas)

    soccer,score,predictor,fixtures,gameweek,office,pool,table,tipping,forecast,results,game,club

93 characters.

Two rules worth knowing. Apple already indexes every word in your name and
subtitle, so repeating football, prediction, league or friends here would waste
the space. And anything gambling-adjacent — bet, odds, stake, sweepstake — is
left out deliberately: it invites a review of whether the app is a gambling
product, which is an argument you do not want to have over a free game.

## 4. Promotional Text (170 characters max)

    Season predictions are open. Write your own questions, lock them before kick-off, and see everyone's calls the moment the deadline passes.

135 characters.

This is the one field you can change any time WITHOUT submitting a new version
or waiting for review. Use it for whatever is live right now — the start of a
season, a cup round, a new feature.

## 5. Description

Only the first three lines show before "more", so everything that matters is at
the top.

    ALOTO Prediction Pro runs the football score prediction league you have been
    keeping in a spreadsheet. Set up a competition, share the join code with your
    group, and the app handles the rest — locking predictions at kick-off, scoring
    every result, and keeping the table up to date.

    HOW IT WORKS
    One person creates a competition and runs it. Everyone else joins with a
    six-character code. Add your fixtures each gameweek, enter the results when the
    matches finish, and the points, tables and bonuses resolve themselves.

    SCORING
    Two points for the correct result, five for the exact score, and bonus points
    for a full house of results or scorelines across a gameweek. Every value is
    yours to change.

    TRIPLE POINTS
    Two chips a season, one either side of New Year. Play one and everything you
    score that gameweek is tripled.

    LEAGUES, CUPS AND TOURNAMENTS
    Run a season-long league, a knockout cup drawn on gameweek scores, or a group
    stage feeding into a bracket. Run all three at once if your group has the
    appetite.

    SEASON PREDICTIONS
    Call the final league table in August and back your picks for the season's
    winners. Everything stays sealed until the deadline, then opens for the whole
    group to compare.

    LIVE SCORES ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
    Your gameweek total updates as the goals go in, without opening the app.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    Competitions are joined by code only. There is no public directory, no global
    leaderboard, and no way for anyone outside your group to find your competition.
    Predictions stay hidden from everyone, including the admin, until each match
    kicks off.

    FREE TO PLAY
    Playing is always free, however many competitions you are in, and there are no
    adverts anywhere in the app.

    ALOTO PRO — £29.99 a year
    For organisers who run more than one competition. Pro adds unlimited leagues
    and cups, individual season predictions including questions you write yourself,
    and a full archive of past seasons. The free plan includes one league, one cup
    and the final table prediction.

    Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

### What changed, and why

The live description currently ends:

    FREE TO PLAY
    Completely free. No adverts, no subscriptions, nothing to buy.

That is no longer true. The same page sells ALOTO Pro at £29.99 and carries an
"In-App Purchases" label under the title, so the listing contradicts itself in
public. Apple treats inaccurate metadata as a 2.3.1 problem, and a customer who
reads it and then meets a paywall has a fair complaint. Fixing this is the one
change here that is not optional.

Two sections are new — season predictions and lock screen scores — because both
shipped and neither was ever mentioned. The Pro section is written to sell to
the person who actually pays, the organiser, and to make clear that players
never have to.
